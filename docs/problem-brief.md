# Problem Brief

## Scope

This repo is based on a broad synthesis of current internet-native user pain points as of **May 6, 2026**.

Instead of pretending to manually verify "100,000+ verticals," the repo uses:

- a large vertical taxonomy
- repeated public internet pain archetypes
- current evidence from recent 2026 reports

The result is a **1,296-problem atlas** that can be searched and extended.

## Current evidence used

### 1. Fraud and scam pressure is still accelerating

The FTC said on **April 27, 2026** that reported losses to scams that started on social media reached **$2.1 billion in 2025**, eight times the 2020 total.

Source:
- [FTC: Reported losses to scams on social media eight times higher than in 2020](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/data-visualizations/data-spotlight/2026/04/reported-losses-scams-social-media-eight-times-higher-2020)

The FTC also reported on **March 10, 2025** that U.S. consumers lost **$12.5 billion** to fraud in 2024, with big growth in job scams, shopping issues, and identity-related complaints.

Source:
- [FTC: New data show a big jump in reported losses to fraud to $12.5 billion in 2024](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2025/03/new-ftc-data-show-big-jump-reported-losses-fraud-125-billion-2024)

The FBI said on **April 6, 2026** that the IC3 received **1,008,597 complaints** in the 2025 report, with nearly **$21 billion** in cyber-enabled losses and heavy pressure from crypto, phishing, extortion, and AI-linked scams.

Source:
- [FBI: Cryptocurrency and AI scams bilk Americans of billions](https://www.fbi.gov/news/press-releases/cryptocurrency-and-ai-scams-bilk-americans-of-billions)

### 2. Support and customer experience friction remains unresolved

Gartner said on **February 18, 2026** that **91%** of service leaders are under pressure to implement AI in 2026, while still prioritizing customer satisfaction, operational efficiency, and self-service success.

Source:
- [Gartner survey of service and support leaders](https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2026-02-18-gartner-survey-finds-ninety-one-percent-of-customer-service-leaders-under-pressure-to-implement-ai-in-2026)

Medallia said on **March 5, 2026** that **66%** of brands believed CX improved, but only **17%** of consumers agreed, with many departments collecting data but failing to act on it.

Source:
- [Medallia 2026 State of Customer Experience report release](https://www.medallia.com/press-release/medallia-releases-2026-state-of-customer-experience-report/)

Qualtrics' 2026 trends page says:

- only 3 in 10 customers give direct feedback
- 92% say good service drives satisfaction
- 73% are already using AI
- only 20% are interacting with customer support agents
- 86% would share more personal data if use was transparent

Source:
- [Qualtrics 2026 Consumer Experience Trends](https://www.qualtrics.com/en-gb/ebooks-guides/customer-experience-trends/)

### 3. Identity abuse and AI-enabled deception are spreading

McAfee's **February 5, 2026** India release describes increasingly realistic scam patterns, deepfakes, and impersonation, including:

- average exposure to 13 suspicious scam messages daily
- 51% reporting money lost to scams
- 1 in 5 reporting a voice-clone scam encounter

Source:
- [McAfee 2026 State of the Scamiverse](https://www.mcafee.com/en-in/consumer-corporate/newsroom/press-releases/2026/20260204-1.html)

TransUnion's **April 8, 2026** fraud update says one in six U.S. consumers lost money to digital fraud in the past year, and highlights stolen cards, third-party seller scams, account takeover, and identity theft.

Source:
- [TransUnion H1 2026 update to top fraud trends](https://newsroom.transunion.com/h1-2026-update-to-the-top-fraud-trends-report/)

Microsoft's **April 6, 2026** security blog documents an AI-enabled device-code phishing campaign using automation and dynamic code generation to compromise accounts at scale.

Source:
- [Microsoft Security: AI-enabled device code phishing campaign](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/security/blog/2026/04/06/ai-enabled-device-code-phishing-campaign-april-2026/)

## Product thesis

The top internet problems are not "one market, one bug."

They are recurring patterns that reappear across:

- ecommerce
- banking and fintech
- healthcare
- education
- social and community platforms
- creator economy
- travel and hospitality
- government services
- SaaS and subscription services

This repo turns those repeating patterns into a structured atlas so founders, operators, product teams, and researchers can:

- spot the highest-value pain clusters
- compare problems across verticals
- find AI and automation opportunities
- avoid building products for shallow or low-frequency pain
